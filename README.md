## ⭐️ What is this project?

**Awaken AI**

Feeding the Machine: Exploring Class Exploitation and Data WorkerUprising in the Al Era

The project highlights the hidden class exploitation in the Al era, where data labelers face low wages (lessthan $2 per hour), grueling work conditions, and mental strain from processing harmful content. While theirlabor improves Al accuracy, it also reduces demand for their roles, creating a self-perpetuating cycle ofexploitation and obsolescence.

Through an immersive plant cultivation game, users can experience this cycle firsthand. Players feed virtualplants with personal data, nurturing them to maturity, only to see the plants consume human resources. Thissymbolizes the exploitation inherent in Al training and hints at the possibility of a "data labor uprising.
The project consists of three core elements:

- A game application to reveal the relationship between A and labor through aamification.

- An interactive plant exhibition to showcase the cost of Al progress;

- A Web3 platform to empower data workers and reimagine the balance between humans, data, and Al.

This initiative aims to uncover Al-era exploitation, advocate for labor rights, and spark dialogue on ethics
labor, and resistance in the digital age.
